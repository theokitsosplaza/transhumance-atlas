Every pasture has one ID and several names, each tagged with its language. Pasture types and seasons come from a small shared vocabulary, so yayla, жайлоо and summer pasture are one concept. Regions point to their Wikidata IDs.

Interviews are shown only by their description (date, language, places mentioned). Recordings stay closed.
